The problem

Site diaries shouldn't live in chat threads and spreadsheets.

On most projects the day's record is a paper diary, a WhatsApp photo dump and an Excel sheet someone fills in at night. By month end, the progress report means days of chasing.

Reports come in late

Engineers are on site all day, often without signal. Writing it up later means numbers get guessed and days get skipped.

Evidence is scattered

Photos without dates or locations, labour counts in one place, quantities in another. Hard to trust, harder to find.

Monthly reports eat days

Compiling the FIDIC monthly progress report by hand means copying a month of diaries into Word, then checking it all again.

How it works

From the site to the client, in one flow.

Everyone works from the same record. Nothing is retyped.

  1. 1Site engineer

    Files the day's report

    Weather, labour, equipment, materials, work done, photos and issues, in a few taps. Copy yesterday's and change only what moved.

  2. 2Project manager

    Approves or sends back

    Review on phone or web, approve or send back with a comment. Approved reports lock; later changes become timestamped addenda.

  3. 3Client and consultant

    Follow along, read-only

    A dashboard or an expiring share link for the resident engineer, client or lender. No login needed, and they can acknowledge reports.

  4. 4Reports

    PDFs compile themselves

    Branded daily reports and the monthly FIDIC progress report, ready to send by WhatsApp, Telegram or email.

Features

Everything the site record needs, nothing it doesn't.

Copy yesterday

Start from the last report. Changed fields are highlighted so the PM sees exactly what moved.

Photos with GPS and time

Camera-first capture, stamped with location and time, tagged to the activity and chainage.

Forms builder

Build your own checklists (concrete pours, inspections, snags) with photos, signatures and GPS. Publish to projects and roles; they work offline.

Issue board

Raise issues and NCRs with priority, owner and due date. Track them from open to closed with before and after photos.

Dashboards

Planned against actual progress, labour and equipment trends, delays, and an automatic dashboard for every form, with Excel export.

Locations and chainage

Blocks and floors, zones, or road and pipeline sections with chainage like Km 12+400. Fits buildings and linear projects alike.

Safety log

Incidents, near misses and toolbox talks. An incident alerts the PM and client straight away.

A record you can trust

Approved reports can't be edited. Every change is an addendum with a timestamp, and every form entry keeps its version.

Sign in with your phone

Phone number and a one-time code. No passwords to forget, and teams are invited by phone number.

Built for the field

Made for the site, not the office.

Designed for low-end phones, patchy networks, bright sunlight and gloved thumbs: big buttons, primary actions at the bottom, minimal typing.

Offline first. Really.

No signal at the quarry? Submit anyway. The report and photos are saved on the phone and send themselves when the network comes back.

  • Reports, photos and forms stored on the phone
  • Clear status: saved on phone, waiting for network, synced
  • New forms download on the next sync and work offline
  • Apps for Android and iOS
